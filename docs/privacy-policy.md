# Isopix privacy policy

@include rules

## What Isopix sends, and what it keeps

- **Sent to us**, while **Share play data** is on (at the bottom of Settings; on to start with), from isopix.app only and never from automated browsers:
- **How Isopix is used:** when it opens, with the window size and display settings; which screens, dialogs, buttons, tools and keys are used, never where on the screen; when a drawing is started, finished or left; settings changed from and to, never the colours you pick; how long a visit lasts; how fast it loads and slow moments.
- **The kind of device:** screen size, touch or not, installed or not, light or dark, reduced motion, and language.
- **What went wrong:** the kind of error and where in the app, never its message.
- **With each record:** the device's random code, which never changes, and the time zone, as its offset from UTC.
- **Not sent:** your drawings, their names, anything you type (except Feedback you send: below), your city or region, or your browser's full identifying string. Your drawings stay in your browser.
- **Cloudflare**, our host, also counts page views, without cookies.

@include feedback

## Deleting your data

- **In your browser:** clear isopix.app's site data. That deletes your drawings too, so export any you want to keep first.
- **Usage data:** turn Share play data off to stop sending more. We can't tell which records are yours: the code is never shown and is linked to nothing else. It is removed after one year anyway.
- **Feedback:** we can't tell which message is yours either. Write to {contact} with roughly what it said and when, and we'll delete it; it is deleted after 90 days anyway.
- **Questions or requests:** write to {contact}.
