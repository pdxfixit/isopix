About

📊 Which buttons get used, never your drawings or what you type.

What is counted, and what never is

📊 To decide what to improve, Isopix counts which buttons, tools, keys and settings are used, how long a visit lasts, and the kind of device (screen size, touch, language, light or dark), with the country the visit came from. Never your drawings, names or anything you type, and never your internet address. Each device gets a random code that never changes, made on the device and linked to nothing else; it is removed after a year from everything Isopix keeps. Isopix keeps them 90 days online and in an archive on its maker's computer, and never sells or shares them. Cloudflare, the host, also counts page views, without cookies.
